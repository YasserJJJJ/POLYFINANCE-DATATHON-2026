 Renewing Canada’s monetary policy framework

Every five years, the Bank of Canada and the Government of Canada review and renew the agreement on Canada’s monetary policy framework. The most recent review concluded with the renewal of the framework for another five years—from January 2027 to December 2031.

 Regulatory oversight

We oversee Canada’s payments and financial market infrastructures to ensure they are safe, resilient and stable. We also support the evolution of the payments ecosystem, including new mandates for stablecoins and consumer‑driven banking.

 Monetary Policy Report—October 2026

Inflation in Canada has been higher than anticipated and is projected to remain around 3% through much of 2027. It then returns close to 2% by the middle of 2028. The outlook for growth has improved, but uncertainty remains elevated.

 Financial Stability Report—2026

Canada’s financial system has functioned well through a challenging year. Households and businesses remain in stable financial condition, and banks have strengthened their capacity to absorb shocks. However, vulnerabilities have increased in some parts of the system, and a more turbulent global environment poses risks to financial stability.

 Forecasting oil prices: What works best and when

Oil price forecasts help central banks assess risks to inflation and economic activity. Forecasters often rely on the latest price of oil, on futures markets or on economic models. The best approach depends on how far ahead forecasters are looking and whether they need only a forecast or also an explanation of what’s behind it.
