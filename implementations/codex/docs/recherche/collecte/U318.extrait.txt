Supported Regions and models for Amazon Bedrock Guardrails

Amazon Bedrock Guardrails applies to foundation models that you invoke on the bedrock-runtime 
 endpoint through the InvokeModel ,
 InvokeModelWithResponseStream , Converse , and
 ConverseStream operations. Within those requests, a guardrail can evaluate
 both text and image content — for image content, see Block harmful images with content filters . The bedrock-mantle endpoint
 doesn't support Amazon Bedrock Guardrails.

Most models in the Amazon Bedrock catalog support guardrails. The following models
 don't.

Point-version model IDs follow their base model. For example,
 amazon.titan-embed-text-v2:0:8k doesn't support guardrails because
 amazon.titan-embed-text-v2:0 doesn't.

A request that references a guardrail with one of these models fails with the
 following error, even though the same request succeeds when you omit the guardrail
 parameters:

 ValidationException: Guardrail is not supported with the chosen model 

Note

Don't infer guardrail support from the model type. Models of the same type can
 differ — TwelveLabs Pegasus v1.2 supports guardrails while Pegasus v1.5
 doesn't — and the Stability AI image generation models do support
 guardrails.

To evaluate content without invoking a model, use the ApplyGuardrail 
 operation. It applies a guardrail to content that you supply directly, so it isn't
 tied to any model and works regardless of which models your application uses.

To confirm support for a specific model, go to models at
 a glance , choose the model, and check the Capabilities and
 Features section of its model card.
