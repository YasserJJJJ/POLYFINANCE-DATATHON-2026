Quotas for Amazon Bedrock

Your AWS account has default quotas, formerly referred to as limits, for Amazon Bedrock. To view
 service quotas for Amazon Bedrock, do one of the following:

Follow the steps at Viewing service
 quotas and select Amazon Bedrock as the service.

Refer to the Amazon Bedrock service quotas in the AWS General Reference.

Model inference in Amazon Bedrock is controlled by quotas on token usage. Some models use tokens at a higher rate. For more information about these rates and how to optimize your token usage, see How tokens are counted in Amazon Bedrock .

Embedding models are an exception: they are throttled by requests per minute (RPM) rather than tokens per minute (TPM). Use the RPM quota when you plan capacity or request a quota increase for an embedding model. For more information, see Amazon Titan Text Embeddings models .

Amazon Bedrock offers two inference endpoints – bedrock-runtime and bedrock-mantle – each with its own per-model quota allocations. Traffic to the two endpoints is tracked against separate quotas, even when calling the same underlying model. For details, see Quotas for the bedrock-runtime endpoint and Quotas for the bedrock-mantle endpoint .

To maintain the performance of the service and to ensure appropriate usage of Amazon Bedrock, the
 default quotas assigned to an account might be updated depending on regional factors,
 account age, usage history, payment history, fraudulent usage, and/or approval of a quota increase request .
 As a result, the quotas that apply to your account might be lower than the default values
 listed in the Amazon Bedrock service quotas . To check the values that apply to your account, use Service Quotas as
 described earlier in this topic.

Quota increases aren't granted automatically. For the request process and the conditions
 that apply to a request, see Request an increase for Amazon Bedrock quotas .
